Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

The Dark History Uploader is a personal automation tool built and operated solely by its developer (Sayamol Petchpraphan) for one purpose: publishing "The Dark History" video content to her own TikTok account. It is not distributed to, or used by, any other person.

What this app accesses

This app requests TikTok OAuth access (via the video.publish and user.info.basic scopes) solely to upload videos to, and read basic profile information from, the developer's own TikTok account. No content, credentials, or personal data belonging to any other TikTok user is accessed, stored, or processed.

What is stored

This app does not run a persistent server or database. Access tokens obtained from TikTok are used immediately to complete a video upload and are not retained afterward. No user data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
